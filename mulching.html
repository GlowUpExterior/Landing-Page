Mulching

Fresh Beds That Finish the Look

A fresh layer of mulch locks in moisture, chokes out weeds, and gives every bed a clean, finished edge.

What's Included

Mulching Service Details

We prep beds properly before mulch goes down, so it looks good and actually does its job.

Old mulch, leaves, and weeds are cleared out first so fresh mulch goes down on a clean bed.

Mulch is delivered and hand-spread across every bed on your property in one visit.

We keep mulch at a consistent depth—thick enough to block weeds, not so deep it smothers plants.

Bed edges are cleaned up before mulching so the finished line looks sharp.

Choose from mulch colors and materials that match your home and existing beds.

Old mulch and any excess material are cleared away—nothing left piled on your driveway or lawn.

How It Works

Our Process

Bed Assessment

We check bed size, existing mulch depth, and drainage before ordering material.

Clean-Out & Edging

Old mulch, weeds, and debris are cleared and edges are cleaned up first.

Mulch Application

Fresh mulch is spread to an even depth across every bed.

Final Walkthrough

We walk the beds with you to confirm coverage and finish.
