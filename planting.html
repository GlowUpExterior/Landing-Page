Planting

New Life for Your Landscape, at Any Scale

Whether you need a bare bed filled in or a few specific shrubs placed, we handle the planting from start to finish.

What's Included

Planting Service Details

We price and plan planting jobs by scope—a handful of individual plants is a different job than filling in an entire bed, and we quote each accordingly.

Bare or sparse beds filled in completely for an established, finished look right away.

Adding a specific number of shrubs or plants to an existing bed, priced and scoped to just that job.

Annuals and seasonal flowers swapped in to keep beds colorful throughout the year.

Soil is loosened and amended before planting so roots can establish quickly.

We space plants for their full-grown size, not just how the bed looks on planting day.

Every new plant is watered in thoroughly at installation to help it establish.

How It Works

Our Process

Plan & Plant Selection

We talk through scope—whether it's a full bed or a set number of plants—and pick what fits your space and light.

Site & Soil Prep

Beds are cleared and soil is amended so plants have a strong start.

Planting

Each plant goes in at the right depth and spacing for healthy, long-term growth.

Watering-In & Care Tips

We water everything in and leave you with simple care guidance to help it establish.
